What should I ask before reserving off-plan?

Request the unit floor plan, specification, price including applicable taxes, payment milestones and expected completion. Ask for an updated unit list before making a decision.

Can I rent out my holiday home?

Ask the developer and your agent about permitted use, any operating agreement and running costs for the specific home. Confirm whether any proposed rental arrangement is allowed before relying on it.

What about Portugal?

Our documented homes are in Spain. If you are looking in Portugal, tell us your preferred region, budget and how you plan to use the home.
