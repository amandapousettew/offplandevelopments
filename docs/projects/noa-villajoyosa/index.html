Beach, airport and transport

Noa is about 500 m in a straight line from the official Playa Centro map pin. Check the walking route from the building. From Alicante Airport, take bus C6 towards Luceros and change to the L1 TRAM towards La Vila Joiosa. Reverse the route for the airport.
