For estate agents

Meet the developers behind selected Costa Blanca projects.

OffplanDevelopments connects Dutch and Swedish estate agents with developers of selected new-build homes on Spain’s Costa Blanca. Tell Amanda or Potros what your client is looking for. We’ll share relevant project information and introduce you to the developer. Your agency stays your client’s point of contact.

Request a developer introduction

How the introduction works

  1. Send a buyer brief

    Tell us the area, budget, property type and timing. You can leave out your buyer’s name and contact details at this stage.

  2. Review the project information

    We share relevant documents and ask the developer to confirm current prices, options and availability.

  3. Meet the developer

    We make the introduction and record which agency and project it relates to. Your agency and the developer take it from there.
